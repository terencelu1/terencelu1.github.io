## The program

An international project-based learning program; most participants had no drone or programming background. I designed a system they could pick up quickly and supported them on site.

## System design

- **Modular, vision separated from control:** Python threads split image processing and flight control, so students could change one without breaking the other.
- **MediaPipe gestures:** fly the drone with hand gestures.
- **QR-code navigation:** PyZbar reads QR codes as mission instructions.
- **DroneKit + Pixhawk:** the Raspberry Pi commands the flight controller through DroneKit.

Teams from each country finished by flying set trajectories with DroneKit in a final competition.

## Teaching across cultures

Participants differed widely in accent and background. I broke concepts into small steps, demonstrated instead of lecturing, and rehearsed repeatedly; I also set up the on-site network and planned the demo flow.
